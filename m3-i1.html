Unit 3 Discussion

You have reviewed online portfolios and profiles of various types. You've studied the latest resume techniques. Now it's time to assess and integrate the information you'v absorbed, and put it to use. 

Answer the following questions. Be specific, citing details from each resource in your answer.

Citing your sources - Web, Blogs, Social Media

 

1. What are your most important takeaways from each of the portfolio examples - write two to three sentences for each: 

  • The two LAS-430 Student Portfolio examples
  • One of the Non-SHU e-Portfolio examples
  • One of the Creative Portfolio examples
  • One of the Professional Portfolio examples

2. Select one of the Creative or Professional portfolio examples from the Reading+, and assess it for strengths and opportunities:  

  • How well does the biography provide a compelling narrative of the individual? How could it be improved? 
  • How does the portfolio/profile detail the individual's knowledge, skills, and experience? How could it be improved?
  • In what way(s) does the portfolio/profile provide samples of the individual's actual work?
  • In what way(s) does the portfolio/profile use multi-media (text/images/audio/video) to engage and inform?

3. How will you use these insights in the development of your e-Portfolio?

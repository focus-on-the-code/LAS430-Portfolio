Unit 1 Portfolio Deliverable

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

Start early, plan ahead, and pace yourself...

The Portfolio Deliverable assignments serve three purposes:  

  • To break down the final project into smaller parts 
  • To ensure your familiarity with the e-portfolio tools 
  • To get feedback on different elements of your portfolio early and often, from your peers and prof.  

 

The Unit 1 Portfolio Deliverable will focus on getting comfortable with the technology, and getting your portfolio started. 

Complete the following actions: 

1. Re-read the Priority 4: Final Assignment Requirements – E-Portfolio

2. Create your E-Portfolio in Canvas

  • Creating an e-Portfolio in Canvas.docx - step-by-step instructions, text only
  • Creating your ePortfolio on CanvasLinks to an external site. - tutorial and tool walkthrough (YouTube)

Check the Learning Resources for more information

3. Create and title all of the sections and pages you will need for the final portfolio: 

  • Home/Landing page
  • Personal Statement/Narrative page
  • SHU & Dominican Mission page
  • Classes & Coursework
    • Liberal Arts Studies Course 1
    • Liberal Arts Studies Course 1
    • Major Course 1
    • Major Course 2
    • Non-Major Course 1
    • Non-Major Course 2

Note: The above titles are just placeholders. Use them now, and change them later to be more descriptive, once you'e started adding content to the portfolio. 

4. On the Home/Landing page, write the first draft of your biography. 

There's no word limit here. Make it clear, concise, descriptive, and have a little fun.

5. Once complete, create a 5-7 minute video walkthrough of your current portfolio. Be sure to include each of the below elements: 

  • Start with your biography, and introduce yourself to the class
  • Click through each page you created
  • Reflect on the process, and share your thoughts: 
    • Have you ever created a Canvas e-Portfolio before? 
      • If you have: when and why did you create the e-Portfolio? What's changed in the tech since you last used it? What do you like/dislike about the Canvas e-Portfolio platform?
      • If not: how was it getting started? What do you like/dislike about the Canvas e-Portfolio platform?
    • Have you ever created a digital portfolio on any platform? 
      • If you have: when and why did you create the portfolio, what platform did you use, and what's different about What do you like/dislike about the Canvas e-Portfolio platform compared to other tools you've used? 
      • If not: why not? After reading the Priority 4: Final Assignment Requirements – E-Portfolio document, do you think you'll create your own portfolio in the future?
  • End your video with two specific, open-ended questions for your classmates to address in their replies. These questions should seek substantiative feedback related to specific content and/or design elements of your portfolio.

6. To submit your deliverable: embed your video, and include a link to your e-Portfolio below the video. Be sure everyone can access the video and portfolio before submitting.

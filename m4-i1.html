Unit 4 Discussion

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

This Assignment has 2 parts. Both are required. 

Part 1: 400+ word Discussion post

Briefly discuss an experience where you or someone you know very well has faced an ethical dilemma at a workplace. You do not need to reveal any details that would be inappropriate or private. Describe the situation, and how you or they decided what to do about it.

Address the following questions, referencing, and accurately citing, specific examples/information from this week's course material. 

  • How do you know what is and is not ethical behavior?
  • By what criteria do you judge the right or wrong of a situation? What factor(s) could change that perspective - culture, country, history, religion?
  • What did you think of the Markkula Center's Ethical Decision Making framework? How has it changed or challenged your thinking on decision making?

 

Part 2: GenAI Experiment & 200+ word reflection – to complete this assignment, you will use a Generative AI chat bot for deep reading.

This assignment will help you learn how LLMs work, and how to use them to deepen reading and enhance understanding.

Follow each of these steps: 

Before using AI

    1. Watch Prof. T's two videos: Deep Reading with AI, and GenAI Assignment & Demo (see Unit 4 Overview).
    2. Select two or more resources on ethical decision making, taken from Unit 4, or from your own research, and read them. You can select any resources you want, for example: 
      • A Framework for Ethical Decision Making
      • Sanders, The Most Human Art: 10 reasons why we'll always need good stories
      • Beth Haile, What is the Common Good? 
      • Jack Sanders - Professional Ethics Lecture #1: Introduction (YouTube)
      • Resources from The Ethics Centre
      • Research and select your own ethics / ethical decision making resources.

AI Experiment

    1. Choose a chat bot: ChatGPT,  Google Gemini, Claude.ai, Perplexity.ai, Microsoft CoPilot (available with your Siena Heights Office 365 subscription). Any of the free chat bots will work.
    2. Upload your selected resources to the chat bot: upload the files, copy-and-paste the text into the chat window, or reference the web page where the content is stored.
    3. Work with GenAI to deepen your understanding of the text over “three (3) turns” (i.e.: three questions from you, three responses from the bot): Here are some examples of questions/commands that I use:

      • What is the main takeaway, in one or two sentences?
      • How are these resources connected? 
      • How are these resources different? 
      • Explain the main concept(s) so that an eight grader would understand.
      • Give me a relatable example of [fill in the blank] concept so I can better understand.
      • Search the web for similar resources that will improve my understanding of [fill in the blank] concept.

      4. Create a Study Guide: use this prompt, or build your own –

“I want you to create a short study guide that will help me deepen my understanding of the specific resources uploaded, and the concepts we’ve discussed. My goal is to understand the concepts well enough to relate them to my own experiences, and explain them to others. The study guide can take any form you think best to accomplish this goal. 

Before you begin, ask me three questions, one at a time, that will help you craft the best study guide for me. Once I’ve answered your questions, create a short study guide.” 

 

200+ word Reflection 

In 200+ words, reflect on your experience: The below questions may help you reflect. You're not required to answer them, talk about anything you want: 

    • How does this experiment compare with your prior use of GenAI chatbots? 
    • What did you think of the chatbot's interpretation of the resources?
    • Did you notice any errors in the chatbot’s interpretation of the resources? 
    • What did you think of the study guide? 
    • Did the conversation with the chatbot help improve your understanding of the material/concepts? Why or why not? 
    • What in this experiment surprised you?

 

To Submit your Discussion post

  1. Part 1 – 400+ word Discussion: post the text of your 400+ word response to the discussion prompt. Be sure you cite all sources referenced, and cite your use of GenAI.
  2. Part 2 – GenAI Experiment (200+ word Reflection & Study Guide): post the text of your 200+ word reflection on your GenAI experiment, and attach two (2) separate documents:
    • The study guide you created.
    • A full transcript of your work with the chatbot.

At the end of the reflection, be sure to add a Works Cited listing the 2+ sources used, and your use of GenAI.

 

Please review the discussion rubric, writing expectations, and GenAI policy before you post. Make sure you adhere to class rules, guidelines, and writing expectations.

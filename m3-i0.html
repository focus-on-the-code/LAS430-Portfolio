Unit 3 Overview

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

In Unit 3 we will continue building our e-Portfolio 

This week we will return to our e-Portfolios and continue to build out the required content. To inspire and improve our creations, we will explore other portfolios and related resources. Examine each of the resources below with a keen eye, identifying the strengths, opportunities, and elements of the portfolios that interest you.

With ever resource and example below, ask yourself: how can I use this information to improve my portfolio project?

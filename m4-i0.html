Unit 4 Overview

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

 

Stories teach us how to be human. We are creatures of instinct, but not solely of instinct. More than any other animal, we must learn how to behave...At the heart of many tales is a test, a riddle, a problem to solve; and that, surely, is the condition of our lives, both in detail -- as we decide how to act in the present moment -- and in general, as we seek to understand what it all means. Like so many characters, we are lost in a dark wood, a labyrinth, a swamp, and we need a trail of stories to show us the way back to our true home. (Scott Russell Sanders p. 58) 

Scott Russell Sanders, The Most Human Art: 10 reasons why we'll always need good stories

 

In this unit students will examine and reflect on personal and professional ethics

Assignments

Reading +

  • Chapter 3 & 5 in Not for Profit
  • A Framework for Ethical Decision Making
  • Beth Haile, What is the Common Good? 
  • Jack Sanders - Professional Ethics Lecture #1: Introduction (YouTube)

Additional material (not required)

  • The Ethics Centre includes tons of excellent resources and background information on ethics.
  • Sanders, The Most Human Art: 10 reasons why we'll always need good stories

Discussion & Replies

  • Address all questions in the Discussion assignment
  • Reply to three classmates' posts

 

Final Reflection

  • Take a moment to reflect on the material we've covered, and the class discussion posts.

 

This Unit Meets the following course outcomes:

  • Students will synthesize ideas, content, and methods of various disciplines 
  • Students will demonstrate information literacy and technical proficiency appropriate for scholarly and professional work 
  • Students will analyze course material (written, visual, oral, presentation, etc.) to elevate critical thinking skills 
  • Students will explore civic and social responsibility 
  • Students will Analyze personal and professional ethics

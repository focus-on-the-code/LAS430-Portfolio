Free Pass on One Assignment

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

Everybody gets one, 'cause life happens.  

One free pass to excuse one Discussion or Reflection assignment in Units 1-6 from your grade. No questions asked.  

It can be redeemed any time prior to submitting your final. You will receive no points for the identified assignment: it won't count for or against your final grade. 

To use your free pass, send me a brief email (aholt1@sienaheights.edu) that specifically includes:  

Subject: Using My Free Pass: LAS-430 Winter 2026 

Body: Prof. T - I'd like to use my free pass to exclude [insert name of assignment] from my grade, originally due [insert original assignment due date]. 

Important Notes: 

  • This CANNOT be used for ANY assignments in Unit 7. The Final Portfolio and Final Reflection will be graded.  
  • This CANNOT be used on ANY Portfolio Deliverable assignments in Units 1-6. 
    • Only the Discussion and Reflection assignments in Units 1-6 are eligible.  
  • Using the free pass to excuse a Discussion assignment also excuses you from the Discussion Replies.  
  • The free pass must be redeemed prior to submitting your final. Once the final is submitted, any unused free pass is voided.  
  • You cannot sell, gift, or transfer your free pass to another student. 
  • Please adhere to the email specifics above. You can add more info to your redemption request, but not less. This makes it easy for me to find and apply.

-Prof. T

Course Overview & Critical Info: Review in Priority Order · WikiPage

Learning Resources: Reading | Analysis | Writing | Recording | Presenting | Canvas

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

The Liberal Arts teaches us three core skills: deep reading, critical thinking, and clear writing. Each week you will be asked to read/watch/listen to something and then respond to/ask questions about that material. Every assignment is geared toward improving these core skills. To do so, you must make time to dive deep into the materials assigned, wrestle with the questions they ask, and those you come up with. And write, edit, and write some more to express your ideas effectively. 

This is not a race. Yes: there are deadlines each week for you to hi. But the entire course is open to you, and all the material is free. Do not rush!

Here are some resources about reading, analysis, writing, and presenting, for your review. Apply what you learn to this course, and all others. 

First and Foremost: 

You, Writing!: A Guide to College Composition - Top recommended resource. Download a copy for yourself! 

Offering practical, step-by-step guidance on the entire writing process, helping students develop stronger academic, professional, and personal communication skills. 

Second:

Productivity Basics - these three tools (a digital calendar, a to-do list, and asking "what's the next action?") will keep you organized, and help you get things done.

Assignment: Unit 2 Reflection

Take a moment to reflect on the material we covered in this unit, and the class discussion posts. 

What resonated with you from the material? How did reading your peers' ideas change your thinking? What lasting knowledge have you gained?

For this assignment, you must respond in a 5-7 minute video presentation. The presentation must include 1 or more slides with key bullet points you will speak to in your presentation.

Reference, and accurately cite, specific examples/information from the week's course material. Be sure to include MLA formatted in-text citations, and a Works Cited list on your slides.

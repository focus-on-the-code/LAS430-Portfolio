Unit 2 Overview

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

In this unit students will explore what it means to be "educated" from different perspectives - for profit, and for democracy

Assignments

Reading +

  • The Foreword, and Chapters 1 & 2 of M. Nussbaum's, Not for Profit: Why Democracy Needs the Humanities.
  • Changing Education Paradigms (YouTube) - animated video adopted from a talk by Sir Ken Robinson at The Royal Society For the Encouragement of Arts, Manufactures and Commerce.
  • Priority 2: Prof. T's Generative AI Use PolicyOriginal Canvas URL: $WIKI_REFERENCE$/pages/gd7a086abef76bbc16449827f9e11de07?module_item_id=g90919f4de90e7ad355c25029837f5bc4 - if you've read this once, then give it a quick review. How LLMs Work – a basic primer on how generative AI & large language models (LLMs) work...mostly.

Writing with AI – ways to use AI as a writing partner (emphasis on partner) 

 

Additional material (not required)

  • The Surprising Power of a “Useless” Liberal Arts Education (YouTube) - The Art of Manliness podcast
    • Interview with awarding winning journalist and author George Anders on the benefits of a liberal arts education, and his 2017 book: You Can Do Anything: The Surprising Power of a "Useless" Liberal Arts Education.

 

Discussion & Replies

  • Address all questions in the Unit 2 Discussion assignment
  • Reply to three classmates' posts

 

Final Reflection

  • Take a moment to reflect on the material we've covered, and the class discussion posts.

 

This Unit Meets the following course outcomes:

  • Students will synthesize ideas, content, and methods of various disciplines 
  • Students will demonstrate information literacy and technical proficiency appropriate for scholarly and professional work 
  • Students will analyze course material (written, visual, oral, presentation, etc.) to elevate critical thinking skills 
  • Students will evaluate the interdisciplinary approach to higher education

Priority 3: Weekly Assignment Requirements - Discussions, Replies, Reflections, Portfolio Deliverables

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

The TLDR: Here’s the rhythm of our class, week over week. Assignments, deadlines, and grading are detailed below. You are expected to know and apply this guidance. You will not be successful if you stop at this summary. 

  • There are seven (7) Units, each one week in length.  
  • A selection of five types of assignments will rotate each week:  
    • Reading+ : text, audio, and video content you will read, review, and analyze 
    • Discussion posts: your analysis and insights on questions related to the assigned content 
    • Replies: unique responses to classmates’ work 
    • Reflections: audio/video recordings that reflect on what you’ve learned 
    • Portfolio Deliverables: assignments specifically designed to build toward your final project 
  • Your week has a steady beat, with all assignments due by 11:30 PM eastern:   
    • Discussion Posts due Tuesdays 
    • Discussion Replies due Thursdays 
    • Reflections or Portfolio Deliverables due Thursdays 
    • Portfolio Deliverable Replies due Fridays 
  • Unit/Week 7 is the exception: 
    • Reflection due Tuesday, February 24 
    • Final Assignment due Thursday, February 26 
    • Final Assignment Extra Credit Replies due Friday, February 27 
  • You can always work ahead, but once a week closes, it’s done.   
  • Late/Missing work will not be accepted for full credit.

Introduction 

This document explains how our class is organized. After reviewing, you will know what’s coming, what’s expected, and when it’s due. The course is structured for consistency: once you understand the pattern, you can focus on producing your best work. I will do my best not to deviate from this pattern, but no promises. I recommend you add key dates and deadlines to your digital calendar. If you don't have one, I strongly recommend you get one (this is a major productivity tool / life hack).   

Everything we do is meant to help you engage with the material, your classmates, and your own thinking. Each unit builds on the one before, giving you opportunities to read deeply, discuss ideas, respond to others, and reflect on what you’ve learned. The full course is open, and I encourage you to work ahead. However, no matter how far ahead you work, you will need to check in throughout the week to complete your assignments, review my feedback, and improve your work week over week.  

Plan accordingly, use your time wisely, and reach out for help – from me and from your classmates. We’re all in this together, and we can all help each other.  

You’re going to do great!  

 -Prof. T  

  

Unit Structure Overview  

This course is divided into seven units. Each unit is one week. Unit 7 – the final week of class – is focused on completing your final assignment. 

 Assignments 

Across Units 1–6, you’ll regularly work with five types of assignments:  

Reading +  

  • Assigned at the start of each unit.  
  • Provide the foundation for your discussions, replies, and reflections.  
  • Includes specific readings from the textbook, and supplemental texts and videos.   
  • Read deeply: take notes, look for connections to other readings and your own experiences.  

 

Discussions 

  • Your original contribution to a class conversation, usually addressing a question specific to the week’s assigned material.  
  • Each post must be a 600+ word response. Treat these with the same rigor as an essay: a clearly structured argument, citing evidence to support your position.  
  • MLA format citations are required when quoting texts, and/or paraphrasing or summarizing ideas that are not your own. Both in-text citations, and a Work Cited list are required. 

 

Discussion Replies  

  • Three (3) written responses to classmates' discussion posts, 200+ words. 
  • Each reply must specifically address key points in your classmates’ discussion post: providing related insights and analysis, addressing questions raised, commentary that adds to the conversation. 
  • MLA format citations are required when quoting texts, and/or paraphrasing or summarizing ideas that are not your own. Both in-text citations, and a Work Cited list are required. You do not need to cite ideas paraphrased from the discussion post you are replying to. 
  • Three (3) replies are required, in any combination of the following:  
    • Replies to three different classmates' discussion posts. 
    • Replies to a classmates' reply on your post. 
    • Replies to a classmates' reply on any post. 
  • Start by replying to classmates who’s posts have few or no replies to ensure we are all included in the conversation. We’re all in this together, so let’s support everyone as best as we can!  
  • NOT Acceptable: A reply to your own discussion post or reply.  
  • Go beyond “I agree.” Add insight, ask a follow-up question, connect their ideas to other material.   

 

Reflections 

  • 5-7 minute audio, video, and/or presentation reflecting the week’s content and discussions.  
  • Summarize what you’ve learned, highlight insights, and raise questions you may have.  
  • The purpose is to provide time and space to think critically about the ideas presented in the content and discussions, integrating new information into your existing knowledge and worldview. 

 

Portfolio Deliverables 

  • Assignments specifically related to the requirements of your final assignment: your e-portfolio. These assignments serve three purposes:  
    • To break down the final project into smaller parts 
    • To ensure your familiarity with the e-portfolio tools 
    • To get feedback on different elements of your portfolio early and often, from your peers and prof.  
  • Each assignment will require you to build elements of your e-portfolio, submit draft content for feedback, and submit a video discussing the work submitted (e.g.: a walkthrough of the e-portfolio, reflection on the draft content).  
  • At the end of the video walkthrough, you must pose two (2) specific, open-ended questions for your classmates to address in their replies. Questions should seek substantiative feedback related to specific content and/or design elements of your portfolio. For example: 
    • NOT Acceptable: “Do you think my portfolio looks good?”  
      • Closed-ended: requires only a “yes” or “no” response 
    • Not specific: does not provide feedback on a specific content or design elements.  
    • Acceptable: “What changes would you make to improve the layout of this page for easier navigation?”  
      • Open-ended: requires more than a “yes/no” response 
      • Specific: requests feedback on the page design 

 

Portfolio Deliverable Replies 

  • Three (3) written responses to classmates' portfolio deliverables, 100+ words 
  • Treat these like peer reviews: replies should address one or more of the feedback requests raised in the video walkthrough, providing specific feedback, insights, and support.  
  • Three (3) replies are required to three different classmates’ portfolio deliverables. 
  • NOT Acceptable: A reply to your own portfolio deliverable post. 
  • Start by replying to classmates who’s posts have few or no replies to ensure we all receive feedback. We’re all in this together, so let’s support everyone as best as we can! 
  • Go beyond “great job.” Provide specific, actionable, respectful feedback to help your classmates improve their portfolio.  

 

Weekly Assignment Deadlines  

Each unit follows a consistent rhythm. Deadlines will follow the Canvas system clock: it is set for the eastern time zone, and will automatically adjust for daylight savings time. Work turned in after the deadline will not receive full credit and/or not be accepted.   

Rhythm of Each Unit 

Reading+, Discussion, and Discussion Replies will be assigned every week. Reflections, and Portfolio Deliverables and Replies are assigned on alternating weeks. 

  • Unit 1 (week 1):  
    • Complete all Reading+ assignments. 
    • Submit your Discussion Post by Tuesday, 11:30 PM eastern.  
    • Submit your three (3) Discussion Replies by Thursday, 11:30 PM eastern. 
    • Submit your Portfolio Deliverable by Thursday, 11:30 PM eastern. 
    • Submit your three (3) Portfolio Deliverable Replies by Friday, 11:30 PM eastern. 
  • Unit 2 (week 2):  
    • Complete all Reading+ assignments. 
    • Submit your Discussion Post by Tuesday, 11:30 PM eastern.  
    • Submit your three (3) Discussion Replies by Thursday, 11:30 PM eastern. 
    • Submit your Reflection by Thursday, 11:30 PM eastern. 
  • Unit 3: repeats Unit 1 
  • Unit 4: repeats Unit 2 
  • Unit 5: repeats Unit 1 
  • Unit 6: repeats Unit 2 
  • Unit 7:  
    • Submit your Final Reflection by Tuesday, February 24, 11:30 PM eastern. 
    • Submit your Final Assignment on-or-before Thursday, February 26, 11:30 PM eastern. No exceptions, no extensions, no late work will be accepted for credit. 
    • Submit your Final Assignment Extra Credit Replies by Friday, February 27, 11:30 PM eastern. No late replies will be accepted for extra credit.  

Working Ahead, Missed Work, Late Work 

  • All weeks are open once the course begins. I encourage you to work ahead.  
  • Work not submitted (missed work), and/or submitted after the deadline (late work) will not be accepted for full credit. 
  • Your e-portfolio must be submitted on-or-before Thursday, February 26, 11:30pm eastern time. No exceptions, no extensions, no late work will be accepted for credit. 

As soon as you become aware of issues that may prevent you from submitting your work, please reach out to me.  

 

Grading Rubric  

Discussions, replies, reflections, and portfolio deliverables will be awarded points based on content, completeness, use of source material, accurate citations, and on-time delivery. Unless otherwise noted, expect assignment grades and feedback to be delivered through Canvas on Mondays.   

Discussions: 10 possible points  

  • Full Points (10):   
    • Posted on time: by Tuesday, 11:30 PM eastern.   
    • Post is 600 words or greater.   
    • Addresses discussion question(s).   
    • Meets course writing expectations, as assessed by the instructor.   
    • Includes references to assigned material. 
    • Includes accurate and correctly formatted MLA citations in-text, and a Works Cited list/page. 
    • If Generative AI is used, it is cited in the Works Cited list/page, and a full transcript of all inputs/outputs is attached as a separate document. 
  • Partial Points (5):   
    • Posted late: between Tuesday, 11:31 PM and Wednesday, 11:30 PM eastern.   
    • Post is fewer than 600 words.   
    • One or more discussion questions not addressed.   
    • Course writing expectations not met, as assessed by the professor. 
    • References to assigned material missing, not cited, or incorrectly cited in-text; and/or a Works Cited list/page is not included. 
    • Use of Generative AI not properly cited and/or transcript is not included. 
  • No Points (0): 
    • Post not submitted, or submitted more than 24 hours late (after Wednesday 11:30 PM eastern).  
    • Content submitted violates academic dishonesty policy, as assessed by the professor. 

 

Discussion Replies: 6 possible points (2 points per reply) 

  • Full Points (2):   
    • Posted on time: by Thursday, 11:30 PM eastern.  
    • Reply is thorough, thoughtful, and respectful.   
    • Reply is 200 words or greater.   
    • Meets course writing expectations, as assessed by the professor.   
    • If Generative AI is used, it is cited in the post, and a full transcript of all inputs/outputs is attached as a separate document. 
  • Partial Points (1):   
    • Reply attempts to be thorough, thoughtful, and respectful.   
    • Reply is fewer than 200 words.   
    • Course writing expectations not met, as assessed by the professor.   
    • Use of Generative AI not properly cited and/or transcript is not included. 
    • NOTE: Reply 3 is capped at 1 point if your discussion post was more than 24 hours late or missing.  
  • No points (0): 
    • Discussion Reply was not submitted. 
    • Content submitted violates academic dishonesty policy, as assessed by the instructor.  

 

Reflections: 6 possible points  

  • Full Points (6): 
    • Posted on time: by Thursday, 11:30 PM eastern. 
    • Reflection submitted is 5-7 minutes recorded audio, video, and/or slide presentation, as determined by assignment requirements. 
    • Slide presentation is visible in the video submitted, and meets assignment requirements for slide content and quantity. 
    • Reflection demonstrates thoughtful engagement with the course materials and discussion posts, as assessed by the professor. 
    • Reflection demonstrates careful consideration of audio/video/presentation delivery, as assessed by the professor. 
    • Where sources are referenced, an accurate and correctly formatted MLA Works Cited page is included. 
    • If Generative AI is used, it is cited in the post, and a full transcript of all inputs/outputs is attached as a separate document.  
  • Partial Points (3): 
    • Reflection is less than 5-minutes recorded audio, video, and/or slide presentation, as determined by assignment requirements. 
    • Slide presentation included in video does not meet requirements for slide content and/or quantity. 
    • Reflection does not demonstrate consideration of the course materials, discussion posts, or audio/video/presentation delivery, as assessed by the professor. 
    • Where sources are referenced, citations are inaccurate, incorrectly formatted, or omitted. 
    • Use of Generative AI not properly cited and/or transcript is not included.  
  • No Points (0): 
    • Reflection Post was not submitted. 
    • Content submitted violates academic dishonesty policy, as assessed by the instructor. 

 

Portfolio Deliverable Assignments: 10 possible points  

  • Full Points (10):   
    • Posted on time: by Thursday, 11:30 PM eastern. 
    • Addresses assignment requirements. 
    • Includes two (2) specific, open-ended questions seeking feedback from your classmates.   
    • Work submitted demonstrates careful consideration of writing expectations and/or audio/video/presentation delivery, as assessed by the professor. 
    • Where sources are referenced, an accurate and correctly formatted MLA Works Cited page is included. 
    • If Generative AI is used, it is cited in the Works Cited list/page, and a full transcript of all inputs/outputs is attached as a separate document. 
  • Partial Points (5):   
    • Posted late: between Thursday, 11:31 PM and Friday, 11:30 PM eastern.     
    • One or more assignment requirements not addressed.   
    • Includes less than two (2) specific, open-ended questions seeking feedback from your classmates.   
    • Work submitted does not meet writing and/or audio/video/presentation delivery quality expectations, as assessed by the professor.  
    • Where sources are referenced, citations are inaccurate, incorrectly formatted, or omitted. 
    • Use of Generative AI not properly cited and/or transcript is not included. 
  • No Points (0): 
    • Work not submitted, or submitted more than 24 hours late (after Friday, 11:30 PM eastern).  
    • Content submitted violates academic dishonesty policy, as assessed by the professor. 

 

Portfolio Replies: 6 possible points (2 points per reply) 

  • Full Points (2):   
    • Posted on time: by Friday, 11:30 PM eastern.  
    • Reply is thorough, thoughtful, and respectful.   
    • Reply is 100 words or greater.   
    • Reply directly responds to one or more of the feedback questions posed in the portfolio deliverable video. 
    • Meets course writing expectations, as assessed by the professor.   
    • If Generative AI is used, it is cited, and a full transcript of all inputs/outputs is attached as a separate document. 
  • Partial Points (1):   
    • Reply attempts to be thorough, thoughtful, and respectful.   
    • Reply is fewer than 100 words.   
    • Reply does not respond to the feedback questions posed in the portfolio deliverable video. 
    • Course writing expectations not met, as assessed by the professor.   
    • Use of Generative AI not properly cited and/or transcript is not included. 
    • NOTE: Reply 3 is capped at 1 point if your discussion post was more than 24 hours late or missing.  
  • No points (0): 
    • Portfolio Deliverable Reply was not submitted. 
    • Content submitted violates academic dishonesty policy, as assessed by the instructor.

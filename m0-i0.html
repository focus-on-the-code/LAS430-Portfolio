Start Here: Welcome To The Class!

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

 

My name is Anthony (Tony) Holt, and go by Prof. T. I'll be your Adjunct Professor for LAS-430 here at Siena Heights. There’s more about me in the Cyber Cafe, where I look forward to learning more about you. 

My goal is to deliver a well organize and engaging course that adds value to you. I want to ensure my expectations for each assignment are clear, consistent, and transparent. You will see key information repeated across multiple documents. This is meant to reinforce crucial points that will lead to your success.   

That being said: my perspective is my own, and I cannot anticipate all needs. If you ever have questions about a policy, assignment, or resource, or feedback that will improve the class, please reach out. I'm responsive via email, and happy to make time for a meeting. The best way to reach me is through my SHU email address: aholt1@sienaheights.edu.

I want you all to be successful, and to continuously improve my teaching, and the delivery of this course.    

 

As you'll see in my Generative AI policy document, I encourage you to use GenAI as a tool to support your learning, not to do your work for you. I expect you to be transparent with your use: citing GenAI as a "co-creator," and providing the full transcripts of your work.

Failure to cite GenAI use correctly is plagiarism, a violation of the academic dishonesty policy, and will be treated as such.  

 

Use the Cyber Cafe channel for chit-chat, and to share resources and tools. I encourage you to reach out to your classmates for help, and offer support to your peers as well. I encourage you to share your appropriate use of GenAI tools with the class, so we can all learn together. Social learning is one of the best ways to learn, and being of service to your peers builds community, and lasting friendships. 

 

Thank You, in advance, for your time, attention, effort, insights, and joy. It is my honor, and my pleasure, that you’ve chosen to be here with me.  

-Prof. T

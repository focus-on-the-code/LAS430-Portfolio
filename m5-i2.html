Unit 5 Portfolio Deliverable

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

Start early, plan ahead, and pace yourself...

The Portfolio Deliverable assignments serve three purposes:  

  • To break down the final project into smaller parts 
  • To ensure your familiarity with the e-portfolio tools 
  • To get feedback on different elements of your portfolio early and often, from your peers and prof.  

 

The Unit 5 Portfolio Deliverable will focus on building 60% to 80% of your portfolio. 

This is the home stretch...

Complete the following actions: 

1. Update your resume, and add it to your e-Portfolio Home/Landing page

  • Make improvements based on the feedback from your Unit 3 draft. 

2. If necessary, update your biography

  • This section should be dialed in, and complete. If not, complete final improvements based on feedback from Unit 3. 
  • Include any relevant media (images/audio/video) that will make your bio engaging. 

3. Update your Personal Statement/Narrative

If you've decided you personal statement/narrative will be text: 

  • Update the 500-700 word personal statement/narrative in a separate document using Microsoft Word, or a similar text editor. The updated draft should reflect feedback captured in Unit 3.
    • How to access Office365
  • Add the updated draft to your e-Portfolio.
  • Include the updated file as an editable .DOCX attachment in the deliverable post.
  • To get full credit on this post, Prof. T must be able to access and edit this file

If you've decided your personal narrative will be a 10-12 minute video:

  • Create your personal narrative video, and embed it into your e-portfolio.
  • Ensure all notes, script, slides, or other artifacts used to deliver your video narrative are stored in a digital file (e.g.: Microsoft Word file, PowerPoint slide deck, etc.).
  • Include the notes/scripts/slides as an editable file attached in the deliverable post. 

4. Draft your SHU & Dominican Mission page

This page should speak directly to the mission, vision, and values of Siena Heights and the Adrian Dominican Sisters (ADS).

For example: you could write 300-words on the SHU and ADS mission/vision/values/pillars, and how/why they’ve impacted your education (aligning it to your narrative). Add pictures or videos of you actively living the mission, along with a text, audio, or video narration that explains the importance of the artifacts. 

5. Update the Classes & Course work pages

At a minimum, two of the six pages should include the course reflection.  

  • Draft each the 300-400 word reflections in a separate document using Microsoft Word, or a similar text editor.
    • How to access Office365
  • Add it to your e-Portfolio.
  • Include the file(s) as editable .DOCX attachment(s) in the deliverable post.
  • To get full credit on this post, Prof. T must be able to access and edit the file(s)

The page for every course in your e-portfolio must have a 300-400 word written reflection that includes: 

  • A brief description of the course in your own words 
  • Your views/feelings about the course prior to engaging in the coursework 
  • An evaluation of how you demonstrated the outcomes of the course  
  • Your views/feelings about the course after you finished 
  • An analysis of how the course adds value to your work and/or life after you graduate (aligning it to your narrative) 

 

6. Once complete, create a 5-7 minute video walkthrough of your current portfolio. Be sure to include each of the below elements: 

  • Start with your biography: note any changes you've made since Unit 3 and why. If no changes were made, keep this section short. 
  • Give an overview of your resume: note any changes you've made since Unit 3 and why. If no changes were made, keep this section short. 
  • Give an overview of your Personal Statement/Narrative: 
    • Briefly outline your thoughts on your liberal arts education, the knowledge and skills you’ve acquired, why they’re important, and how they will benefit you in the future. 
    • If you selected a framing question, which one did you chose, and why? If not: why not? 
    • Note any changes you've made since Unit 3 and why.
    • If you changed the overview format from text to video, tells us why: what led to that decision? How does the new format improve your personal statement/narrative, and portfolio overall? 
  • Review the SHU & Dominican Mission page. Briefly discuss how the mission, vision, and values have impacted your education. 
  • Review the updated Classes & Coursework pages. Highlight key points from the reflections that speak to how each class is aligned to your personal statement/narrative.
  • End your video with two specific, open-ended questions for your classmates to address in their replies. These questions should seek substantiative feedback related to specific content and/or design elements of your portfolio.

6. Embed your video,  attach your updated Personal Statement/Narrative draft, the two class reflections, and add a link to your e-Portfolio to submit your post. Be sure everyone can access the video, document, and portfolio. 

 

Please review the discussion rubric, writing expectations, and GenAI policy before you post. Make sure you adhere to class rules, guidelines, and expectations.

Unit 5 Discussion

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

For this assignment, you will let your creativity shine, and reflect on its importance. Complete all actions below. 

1. Chose one of the creative activities below. Complete both the creative experiment, and the reflection questions.

  • Found Poetry Experiment
  • Creative Life Collage

Note: both activities require you to select newspapers, magazines, and other periodicals to work with. Two key points:

  • You do not need to buy any newspapers, magazines, or periodicals to complete this assignment. Get creative! The world is swimming in new, old, free, and discarded newspapers and magazines: office waiting rooms and lobbies, coffee shops, restaurants, libraries, bookstores, convenience stores, gas stations, your home, your friends home, the dorm lounge, SHU Faculty & Staff offices. Go ask someone for anything they're not using / won't miss / plan to recycle. 
  • In the spirit of Not For Profit Chapter 6, I encourage you to select materials that you wouldn't normally read. 

2. Complete the creative activity, and insert an image of your completed artifact into your discussion post. Be sure to identify which activity you completed. 

3. In 200+ words answer the Reflection Questions specific to the activity you completed.

  • There's no right or wrong answer. Reflect on how the assignment actually did or did not impact you.

4. In 400+ words, answer the below questions. Reference, and accurately cite, specific examples/information from the Unit 5 resources throughout your response. 

  • How did one or more of the videos on creativity inspire you and/or your work? If none of the videos inspired you, what does inspire you? 
  • How can creative practices help us cultivate compassion for others? If you don't agree, how can we better cultivate compassion for others? 
  • How do the ADS Mission, Vision, and Pillars relate to, creativity and compassion? If you think they're unrelated, tell us why. 

 

Please review the discussion rubric, writing expectations, and GenAI policy before you post. Make sure you adhere to class rules, guidelines, and expectations. 

"Citizens cannot relate well to the complex world around them by factual knowledge and logic alone. The third ability of the citizen...is what we can call the narrative imagination. This means the ability to think what it might be like to be in the shoes of a person different from oneself, to be an intelligent reader of that person’s story, and to understand the emotions and wishes and desires that someone so placed might have. The cultivation of sympathy has been a key part of the best modern ideas of democratic education...a central role in the curriculum to the humanities and the arts, cultivating a participatory type of education that activates and refines the capacity to see the world through another person’s eyes." 

Nussbaum, 95-96

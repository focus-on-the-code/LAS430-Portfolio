Instructional decisions

Student-facing clarity

Course policies and task requirements were collected into a priority-based orientation so online students could find expectations and resources independently.

Feedback before final grading

Intermediate deliverables made room for portfolio walkthroughs, instructor guidance, classmate responses, and iteration.

Academic and practical synthesis

Students combined research and critical reflection with a digital portfolio, written narrative, and multimedia presentation.

Teaching outcome

100% of final portfolios completed on time

Instructor-reported outcome: all required final portfolio elements were delivered. The course export supports the teaching-design evidence, not an independent audit of student submissions.

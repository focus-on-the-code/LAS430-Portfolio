Tech Resources: OFFICE 365

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

Siena Heights University (SHU) provides all student access to Office 365, Microsoft's online productivity suite, for school use. This includes Outlook (email), Teams (messaging, video calls, real-time collaboration tools), Word (text editor), OneDrive (cloud storage), Excel (spreadsheets), PowerPoint (slides), and other tools we will use in this course. Below you will find instructions to access Office 365.

For additional IT support, contact the IT Help Desk. 

You can access Office 365 in two ways: 

From your MySiena homepage (the easy path)

  1. Login to MySiena
  2. Click the Office.com link on the right (see image below)
  3. If prompted to log-in, use your Siena Heights email and password to do so (as shown in the video below).

Cyber Cafe: Chit-Chat Channel

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

Welcome to the Cyber Cafe: Chit-Chat Channel! 

This discussion board is for general chit-chat among the class. Use this space to introduce yourself, and converse with your classmates:

  • Ask questions about the assignments
  • Trade ideas and resources
  • Ask for help when you're stuck
  • Problem solve together
  • Post things that interest you, and that you think the class would find interesting

We're here learn together, to make connections, and build friendships that foster community.

Share to your level of comfort. Be respectful of your classmates. Be responsible in your choice of topics and conversations. Be engaged with our online community. And most of all, have fun!

-Prof. T

 

 

Extra Credit Opportunity: For those that do post and engage in this channel respectfully and responsibly, I will award extra credit points by the end of the semester. 

Resource links: Canvas-only links and embedded third-party materials may not open in this public teaching portfolio. The original assignment wording and structure are retained where available; no student records are published.

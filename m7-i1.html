Assignment: Unit 7 Final Reflection

Congratulations on making it to the end of this course! Only one more assignment to go: The Final. I look forward to reviewing your portfolios.

For now, take a short break, and reflect back on this class, your work, and our time together.

Post a 5-7 minute audio/video clip and tell me: if you could send a message to yourself on the day this class opened, what would you say? 

  • What's one thing you know now, that you wish you knew then? 
  • What's one thing you'd do differently to ensure your success?
  • What's one assignment / reading / video that you'd tell yourself to look forward to?

Lastly: what's one thing I can I do different, to help my next class succeed? 

No slides required for this reflection.

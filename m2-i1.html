Unit 2 Discussion

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

This Assignment has 2 parts. Both are required. 

Part 1: 400+ word Discussion post – choose one topic below for the focus of your discussion post

Topic 1: Education for Democracy

Review the abilities needed in a "people-sensitive democracy," listed by Nussbaum on pages 25-26, and reflect on them in this discussion. Then address the following questions, referencing, and accurately citing, specific examples/information from this week's course material. 

  • Which of Nussbaum's "abilities" resonate with you, if any? Why, or why not? 
  • Does society need the Humanities? Why or why not?
  • In what ways does the mission and vision of Siena Heights University or the Adrian Dominican Sisters connect to Nussbaum's "people-sensitive democracy?" If not, why not?

 

Topic 2: "Radical Changes" in Education

Briefly describe who, what experiences, and which cultural influences (ethnic background, military family, religion, rural/urban) have shaped your views of education. Then address the following questions, referencing, and accurately citing, specific examples/information from this week's course material. 

  • In Not for Profit, Nussbaum cites 5 examples of "radical changes" in education that she claims threaten to limit our future (pg 3-6). Reflect on these examples.
  • In the video, Sir Ken Robinson discusses the need to change education paradigms. How has your perspective, understanding, and/or experience of education changed over the years?
  •  How will you use the knowledge and skills gained from your liberal arts education to "surf" the waves of academic and social change into the future? 

 

Part 2: GenAI Experiment & 200+ word reflection – to complete this assignment, you will use a Generative AI chat bot as a writing partner

This assignment will help you learn how LLMs work, and how to use them ethically as writing partners.

Follow each of these steps: 

Before using AI

  1. Write your discussion post based on the prompt you selected above. This is 100% your writing.
  2. Watch Prof. T's two videos: How LLMs Work, and Writing with AI (see Unit 2 Overview).

AI Experiment

  1. Choose a chat bot: ChatGPT,  Google Gemini, Claude.ai, Perplexity.ai, Microsoft CoPilot (available with your Siena Heights Office 365 subscription). Any of the free chat bots will work.
  2. Upload your text to the chat bot: copy-and-paste the text of your discussion post into the chat bot text window.
  3. Work with GenAI to improve your writing, over "3 turns:" ask it three questions about your writing, or give it three commands to help you improve your writing. Here are some examples of questions/commands that I use: 
    • Is the writing clear?
    • How can I improve this?
    • Give me feedback to improve this writing.
    • Help me make this idea stronger.
    • What am I missing?

200+ word Reflection 

In 200+ words, reflect on your experience: The below questions may help you reflect. You're not required to answer them, talk about anything you want: 

  • How does this experiment compare with your prior use of GenAI chatbots? 
  • What did you think of the chatbot's feedback? 
  • Did the feedback help improve your discussion post? Why or why not? 
  • What in this experiment surprised you?

 

To Submit your Discussion post

Post your final work: post the final text of your discussion (part 1) and reflection (part 2). Be sure you cite all sources referenced, and cite your use of GenAI.

  • If you choose to to use any of the output to improve your discussion post, make sure you review and edit the content closely. The final work should be yours. 
  • As required by our class GenAI policy: attach a separate document that includes the full transcript of your work with the chat bot. 

 

Please review the discussion rubric, writing expectations, and GenAI policy before you post. Make sure you adhere to class rules, guidelines, and writing expectations.

Unit 5 Overview

Course artifact — adapted for public review from Canvas export

In Unit 5 we will: 

1. Continue Building our e-Portfolio

2. Examine the value of creativity as part of the liberal arts

This week we will return to our e-Portfolios and continue to build out the required content. 

We will also flex our creative muscles, with a few videos and exercises designed to get you thinking differently.

This Unit meets the following outcomes:

  • Students will synthesize ideas, content, and methods of various disciplines 
  • Students will demonstrate information literacy and technical proficiency appropriate for scholarly and professional work 
  • Students will analyze course material (written, visual, oral, presentation, etc.) to elevate critical thinking skills 
  • Students will examine diversity and interdependence of cultures 
  • Evaluate the interdisciplinary approach to higher education 
  • Examine diversity and interdependence of cultures 
  • Demonstrate a sense of social and civic responsibility that supports (aligns with) the Dominican tradition
